## 자세한 설명 (1666/4000)

```
Beating the dealer isn't enough. What matters is how big you win.

LUCKY 21 is a single-player card game that combines blackjack with a deckbuilding roguelike.
Beat the dealer to score your cards' chips × mult, and pass the target score of all 8 stages to win.

■ Blackjack with a deck you design
· You draw from your own deck, separate from the dealer's.
· Cut the cards you don't need, add the ones you want, and enhance them with Gilded, Glass, Gem and Lucky.
· See your bust chance for the next hit, calculated from what's left in your deck.

■ 39 charms that rewrite the scoring formula
· Combine charms like "+3 Mult per heart card" or "×7 Mult when you make 21 with 3+ cards" to build your own combo.
· Charms trigger left to right, so their order is part of the strategy.

■ 5 single-use trick cards
· Peek at the next card, pull a 10 to the top, swap your last card — a trick to get out of a tight spot.

■ 10 bosses that flip the table
· The dealer stands only on 18, hearts get sealed, doubling is banned. Every boss needs a different plan.

■ 5 starting decks and a collection
· A deck full of hearts, a High Roller deck with no 2–5, a Gilded deck, a Tycoon deck.
· Complete achievements to unlock new decks and charms.

■ And more
· Endless mode after clearing stage 8
· In-game tutorial for first-time players
· Demo mode that starts with a finished build
· Continue anytime, even if you close the app
· 7 languages: English, 한국어, 日本語, 繁體中文, Español, Português, Deutsch
· No ads, no in-app purchases, plays offline

※ This game involves no real money or prizes. In-game gold is a virtual currency used only in the shop and cannot be exchanged for cash.
```
